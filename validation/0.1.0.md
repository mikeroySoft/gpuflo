## Skipped checks and limitations

- Workload perturbation (≤ 2 % budget): still unmeasured; no repeatable
  representative workload harness exists on the live host. Accepted for this
  release; it remains the prerequisite for claiming discrete RDNA fully
  qualified rather than a blocker for publication.
- AMD SMI enrichment against a real `libamd_smi.so`: not exercised live;
  covered by the compiled fake-library ABI tests.
- aarch64: no artifact, no support claim.
